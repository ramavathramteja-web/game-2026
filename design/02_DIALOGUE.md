# §4 COMPLETE DIALOGUE SCRIPT

**~120 lines. Every line here is final.** Additions are scope debt, not features.

Format: `RAY:` = first-person internal monologue, non-interactive, plays over movement. `NAME:` = spoken, waits for input to advance. `PLACARD/PANEL/OBJECT:` = readable world text. `[BRACKET]` = direction/staging, not spoken.

**Rules:** no line over two sentences · Ray never explains the joke · Ray never says "memory" until §G · Ray's deduction in §C2 is genuine and must be delivered without winking.

---

## §A — BEAT 1: THE ANNEX

*[Black. A switch click. A torch beam, unsteady, finds a wall.]*

**RAY:** Nine fourteen. Every clock says nine fourteen.
**RAY:** So the Sun is gone, and they gave the job to me, and let's be clear who's winning here. I am.
**RAY:** The last hour is soft. Not missing. Soft. There's a difference and I'm being very generous about it.

*[Player looks around. Torch already on.]*

**RAY:** Right. Torch. Not mine, apparently.

*[Placard 1 — MODULE 1: LIGHT IS EVIDENCE]*
**PLACARD:** TAKE THE LAMP IN YOUR HAND. IT IS NOT YOURS. TAKE IT ANYWAY.
**RAY:** Not mine. Sure. It's in my hand though, so there's a legal argument.

*[Switch bank: A, B, C, D. Placard 2.]*
**PLACARD:** THROW A AND B.
*[Player throws A. Nothing happens.]*

**RAY:** Nope.

*[Player throws B. The door grinds open.]*

**RAY:** A team. A and B. Of course. Why would a thing need a team.

*[Placard 3 — a symbol: a circle with a dot.]*
**PLACARD:** SUNWRITING IS INVISIBLE UNTIL THE LIGHT IS AGREED UPON. HOLD YOUR BEAM ON THE MARK.
*[Player holds the beam on the far wall. Text glows out in loose handwriting:]*

`9:14`

**RAY:** Nine fourteen. Yes. Everyone keeps saying nine fourteen.
**RAY:** Alright. Write it down, Ray.
*[The notebook is written in for the first time, in Ray's handwriting.]*

*[Logbook on the lectern. Ray signs page twelve. The page is blank.]*

**RAY:** Might as well. Somebody will want it signed.

*[Optional — read page twelve first]*
**RAY:** Twelve pages and the twelfth is a lovely clean nothing. Somebody's idea of a joke.

*[Certificate plaque: RAY. Beneath it, an unsoldered nameplate.]*

**RAY:** Somebody was here before me and got their name off. Cowardly, honestly. I respect it.

*[The swivel mirror, already at an angle.]*

**RAY:** Why's the mirror like that? Nobody sets a mirror like that.

*[The city door: dead. A hand-crank service lift.]*

**RAY:** Street, then people. People are easy — they have opinions and not one of them is evidence.

## §B — BEAT 2: DARK CITY

*[One street. Clocks. Moonlight. The single surviving shutter is open.]*

**RAY:** The city didn't lose the light. Something took it and shoved it on the way out.

*[The blown bulb, examined.]*

**RAY:** One bulb on this whole street didn't just go out. It went *outward*. That's not a power cut. That's a push.

**RAY:** Darkness hides things. Good. Darkness is the only thing in this city that isn't on fire with questions.

*[Neon, dead but readable:]*

`SUN'S OUT? WE'RE STILL OPEN`

**RAY:** Brave. Not correct, but brave.

*[Optional — window reflection]*

**RAY:** That's me. No light on me. I'd like to think about that later.

## §C1 — THE MOON

*[Courtyard. The Moon is the only light source: cold, silver, silver enough to be a silhouette and nothing else.]*

**RAY:** You're the Moon.

**MOON:** Yes.

**RAY:** Nine thirteen. Was the Sun up?

**MOON:** Yes.

**RAY:** Nine fourteen?

**MOON:** No.

**RAY:** That's the whole event?

**MOON:** That is the entire event. Please stop walking around it.

**RAY:** Right. One more thing, because you'll get asked badly. The light didn't fade. The light left. Did anything come with it?

**MOON:** Every shadow in this city swung east at once. A hundred thousand people, all turning to look at the same thing.

**RAY:** East. Toward the Sun Control Facility.

**MOON:** Toward where the light *was*. Shadows point at the thing you lost.

**RAY:** Then he wasn't running away from anything. He was running *to* something.

**MOON:** People run toward explosions. It is the entire reason they are still alive.

**RAY:** You're annoyingly encouraging for a rock.

**MOON:** I am a philosopher. Encouragement is a side effect.

## §C2 — MR. WICK, THE TALKING LAMP

*[The one open shop. Wick is lit. He is the only warm thing in the city.]*

**RAY:** You're the suspect.

**WICK:** I am a lamp in a shop. Yes.

**RAY:** What do you see?

**WICK:** Precisely what has been pointed at me. People think that because I'm on, I see. I see the counter. I see the ceiling tile with the water stain.

**RAY:** Did you see anyone come in this morning?

**WICK:** No.

**RAY:** You don't have a door.

**WICK:** I do not. So that was never impressive. Thank you.

**RAY:** Fine. Which way was he going?

**WICK:** East. And I did not see him because he walked *behind* me, and I am not allowed to turn. It is in my contract.

**RAY:** You're a lamp. I'll allow it.

*[Player examines Wick's filament: blown outward.]*

**RAY:** Your filament's blown outward. So is the street bulb. Same push, same direction, everything took it in the face.

**WICK:** Correct. Finally.

---

## §C3 — SIR, THE SUSPICIOUS TOASTER

*[Back of the diner. Silent. A nameplate: SIR.]*

**RAY:** It has a nameplate.
**RAY:** Sir.
**RAY:** Of course.

*[Torch on the toast face. Not damage — a burnt photograph: a wave, entering from the right.]*

**RAY:** That's not burnt. That's a *photograph*. Somebody put an electrical surge on a piece of bread and the bread did the only honest thing available to it.

**RAY:** East. Same direction as the shadows. Whatever did this is east of here.

*[Sir pops a slice. Old, pale, well-done.]*

**RAY:** That one's old. Eleven days old. Sir, you've been keeping the *first* one.

*[Optional — the drawer: eleven slices, one per day, each worse.]*

**RAY:** Eleven days, one a day, each one worse. That's not a diagnosis, Sir. That's a *hobby*.

## §C4 — THE CLOUDS

*[Sky. No movement.]*

**RAY:** Were you moving at nine fourteen?

**CLOUDS:** We were told to hold.

**RAY:** Who told you to hold?

**CLOUDS:** The man who was already up there.

**RAY:** ...Define "up".

## §D — MARLOW, THE VILLAIN

*[Under the dead lighthouse beacon. He starts talking before the player arrives.]*

**MARLOW:** I DIDN'T DO IT.

**RAY:** You haven't heard the question.

**MARLOW:** I DON'T NEED TO. EVERYBODY COMES DOWN HERE AND ASKS. I DIDN'T DO IT.

**RAY:** What is "it"?

**MARLOW:** *[beat]* THAT'S WHAT I'VE BEEN SAYING.

*[He sits. This is the first time he has been quiet in eleven days.]*

**MARLOW:** Eleven days ago I cut the Sun for four seconds. Just to see. It came back. It always comes back.

**RAY:** So you did do it.

**MARLOW:** I DIDN'T DO *IT*. Do you understand the words? Four seconds. There is no "it" after four seconds. There's only *after*.

**RAY:** Then someone finished what you started.

**MARLOW:** Someone signed something. I've been sat here eleven days trying to think of a name and there's only one person in this city who gets to sign things, and he's upstairs pretending to be a person.

**RAY:** That's not a real name, Marlow.

*[Ray walks out. He does not react. The game does not explain why. Four seconds of silence.]*

*[After the puzzle — the counter, the stamps:]*

**MARLOW:** I've tried that. Eleven days, I've tried that. It won't take my light.

**RAY:** ...It took mine.

## §E — BEAT 5: THE GATE

*[Four receiver plates on the gate. Nothing on them.]*

**RAY:** Nothing. Nothing, nothing, nothing. There's something written here and it won't admit it.

*[Player holds the beam on each receiver. Numbered shapes render: triangle, circle, bar, dot.]*

**RAY:** Shapes. Not letters. Somebody didn't want this read by anyone who'd recognise the alphabet.

*[Rotating the relay mirror, making the light bridge.]*

**RAY:** Light goes where you send it. Light is evidence and light is also a crowbar.

*[Four switches, thrown in glyph order. Gate opens. A voice from an unlit room.]*

**LUMEN:** I'm on. I've always been on.

**RAY:** You're... on? Nobody's touched a switch down here in eleven days.

**LUMEN:** No. Nobody has to.

## §F — BEAT 6: THE LAMP VAULT

**LUMEN:** You've been asking who was here.

**RAY:** I have.

**LUMEN:** Ask me properly and I'll answer it. You have not asked me properly once.

**RAY:** ...When?

**LUMEN:** Nine thirteen, forty-one.

**RAY:** Describe him.

**LUMEN:** He was not in a hurry. That's the part I won't be able to explain to you afterwards. He was not in a hurry and he was afraid, and he said "thank you" to a lamp.

**RAY:** That's not a description, that's a mood.

**LUMEN:** You are holding the badge number that let him in. You do not need to read it out. I've watched a hundred people read it out.

*[Ray does not read it out. He moves on to the switch.]*

*[The real Sun Control switch. Torch held. Counter, stamps, a fresh thumbprint.]*

**RAY:** Two. Same as his. Except mine is eleven minutes old and his is eleven *days* old.

**RAY:** That's my thumb. I'd know it anywhere. That's a terrible thing to be able to say.

*[The access log tape. Sunwriting. Ray's handwriting.]*

> **HELLO, RAY. IF YOU ARE READING THIS, I HAVE FORGOTTEN, AND YOU WILL WANT TO KNOW WHY. — R.**

**RAY:** ...

**RAY:** That's my handwriting.

**RAY:** That's my handwriting, and I wrote it, and I don't remember writing it.

**LUMEN:** You said thank you to a lamp.

**RAY:** I did?

**LUMEN:** You said it to me eleven times since. You're saying it now.

*[Custody Transfer panel — read aloud:]*

**PANEL:** A SHUTDOWN HOLDS ONLY WHILE A CASE IS OPEN. IT RELEASES WHEN THE CASE IS CLOSED BY A DETECTIVE WITH NO MEMORY OF CLOSING IT.

**RAY:** Ah. Ah, no.

## §G — BEAT 7: THE MEMORY / THE SWITCH

*[The Annex, again. Reconstruct the light path. Each restored element triggers a fragment.]*

**M1 — [the log, line twelve]** *(Marlow's voice, off, tired)*
**MARLOW (MEMORY):** It's all there. Line twelve says why. —————

**M2 — [the grid schematic]** **RAY:** A restart pushes the east feed first. Nine fourteen in the morning. That's every office, every school, every café in this city, and they're all awake.

**M3 — [the Custody panel]** **RAY:** ...Open case. *[He pulls his badge.]* There. Somebody has to be holding it.

**M4 — [writing page twelve]** **RAY:** It is not a trick. The case has to be closed by someone who did not close it. I am sorry. Also line twelve is still on the wall. Use the light.

**M5 — [9:13:58]** **LUMEN (MEMORY):** Good morning.
**RAY (MEMORY):** Good morning, Lumen. Thank you.
*[The throw. Black. 9:14:00.]*

**RAY:** I turned it off.

**RAY:** I turned it off to stop the restart from killing everybody who was awake.

**RAY:** And then I made myself forget, and I sent myself out here to find out who did it.

**RAY:** That's the worst thing I've ever heard. And I respect it enormously.

*[The panel: RESTORE REQUIRES CLOSED CASE.]*

**RAY:** You close it. Not me. I don't want to be the one who knows.

*[Player connects SIGNATURE + HANDWRITING + COUNTER on the switch. The case closes.]*

**RAY:** Case closed.

*[Ray throws the Sun Control switch. Light. Sound. Every clock resumes at 9:14:01.]*

**RAY:** Nine fourteen and one second.

*[Ray's wristwatch: 9:55. Everyone else's: 9:14:01.]*

**MOON:** *[quietly, from a very long way up]* Ah.

**WICK:** Oh. That's a lot.

**CLOUDS:** *[moving, finally]* Sorry. We're late. We're not late any more.

*[Marlow walks out into daylight. He does not speak. He does not have to.]*

**LUMEN:** *[quietly]* Thank you.

*[Final shot: the Annex wall, in daylight. Sunwriting legible for the first time:]*

`9:14 — R.`
`IT WAS ME.`

*[Final card: Log Entry 914. Line twelve is still black.]*

**RAY:** *[voiceover, last line]* ...Get some sleep, Ray.

*[Screen: `9:13:41.` Black.]*
